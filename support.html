BakeTempo Support

BakeTempo helps bakers who take orders follow each order from the first note to pickup: add orders, see what each batch needs, record what you’ve made, pack each customer’s box and record the pickup.

Update in preparation: the 5-free-orders plan described here applies to the update we are preparing. Your current TestFlight installation may still be an earlier build without this allowance. Pro subscriptions and the free trial are not available yet.

Getting started

  • Start with your own bake. BakeTempo opens in My bake: empty on a new installation, or showing the data you already saved. Demo data appears only when you choose a sample; it never becomes part of My bake.
  • What’s saved. My bake is saved on this iPhone. The optional sample is in Settings. Its changes last until the app closes and do not use your free order allowance. Choose Back to My bake to return to your own data.
  • Five tabs. Today shows the current batch at a glance; Orders lists orders by pickup day; Bake shows what each item needs and has made; Products is your menu; Settings holds language, Pro, purchases, backups, help and policies. Tapping an order opens its Pack screen.
  • Language. Open Settings → Language and choose Follow system, English or 简体中文. Your choice is kept after the app closes. Names and notes you enter are never translated.

Everyday tasks

  • Add a product: Products, then +. Give it a name, a short specification, an illustration and, if you like, default steps for new batches.
  • Add an order: + on Today or Orders. Enter the customer, batch, pickup time, items and quantities, then Save. Your first 5 successfully saved new orders each month are free; Pro is needed for additional new orders. A copy counts as a new order.
  • Record what you’ve made: Bake, then an item, then Record made. If a total is wrong, use Correct made total.
  • Steps: Bake, then an item, then Steps to open its checklist and tick steps off. Ticking a step never changes made counts.
  • Pack a box: Orders, tap the order, then Fill remaining (or enter counts) and Mark packed. You can pack in several rounds.
  • Pickup: once everything in an order is packed, Record pickup records the whole order as picked up. Pickup is for the whole order only. If you recorded it by mistake, use Correct pickup record.
  • Change an order: on its Pack screen, tap Edit. If the order changes after packing has started, it shows Needs review; open Review changes and check the box against the order. Other explicit actions on the Pack screen are Correct packed counts, Correct customer name and Cancel order. Taking items out of a packed box asks you to confirm before anything is saved.
  • Finished batches: Orders, then Archive batch when every order is finished or cancelled. Archived batches are read-only under Archived batches; you can copy an order from there into new work.
  • Share a list: Bake, then Share production list, or Share on a Pack screen for that customer’s packing list. Lists are plain text and go only where you choose in the share sheet.

Backups: please keep your own

BakeTempo has no account and no automatic cloud sync. Your data is on your iPhone, so back it up.

  • Export: open Settings → Backups → Export backup, and choose where to save the JSON file (for example in Files). The file contains all of My bake, including customer names and notes, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Import: Backups, then Import backup, and pick a backup file. BakeTempo shows what the file holds; nothing changes until you choose Replace My bake. The app keeps a copy of your previous My bake inside the app, but that copy is not a backup you can take elsewhere.
  • Moving to a new iPhone: export on the old iPhone and import on the new one. Your bakery data moves only with a backup file, or with your own full device backup and restore.
  • Device backups: your iCloud Backup or computer backups may include BakeTempo’s data if you have them turned on. They are managed by Apple or your computer, not by BakeTempo.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the iPhone. Exported files, shared lists and device backups are not removed.
  • BakeTempo’s developer cannot see your data, so lost data cannot be recovered for you.

BakeTempo Pro and the free trial

  • Free and Pro: in the update being prepared, you can create up to 5 real orders in My bake for free each calendar month. Pro is planned to let you add and copy orders beyond the free allowance. You can manage your product menu without Pro.
  • Planned price: one auto-renewable monthly subscription at US$9.99 per month in the United States. Pro subscriptions are not available yet. When they become available, the app and Apple’s confirmation screen will show the actual price for your country or region before you subscribe.
  • Planned free trial: a 3-day free trial for eligible Apple Accounts, followed by renewal at the monthly price. The free trial is not available yet. When it becomes available, Apple will determine eligibility and show the actual offer before you confirm. Installing the app does not start a trial. Each Apple Account can use only one introductory offer per subscription group.
  • Avoiding renewal once subscriptions are available: to avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. An active subscription renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Without Pro, or after it ends, you can still see every saved order and archived batch; finish saved orders (made, steps, packing and pickup, and their protected changes); share lists; and export, back up or restore My bake. Nothing is deleted when Pro ends. The free allowance applies to new orders.
  • Where to find it: open Settings → View BakeTempo Pro. Settings also provides Restore purchases and Manage subscription. The Pro page links to the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use (Apple Standard EULA).

How the 5 free orders work

A free place is used only when a new order is successfully saved without an active Pro subscription or trial. Copies count as new orders. Abandoning a draft, a failed save or saving the same order again does not use another place. Cancelling or archiving a saved order does not return a used place.

The allowance renews each calendar month, using the time zone saved when the allowance first starts. Settings shows the remaining allowance and its next reset date. Changing an order’s pickup date, your language or your device’s time zone does not grant extra places. Unused places do not carry over.

Orders created while Pro is active do not use free places. If Pro ends, you can use the free places that remain for that month and finish your saved work. Restoring an older backup on the same installation does not reset the used allowance. Bakery data and the allowance are local; there is no account-based cross-device sync.

Restore purchases

On the Pro page, tap Restore purchases while you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Apple checks its records; if Apple reports an active BakeTempo Pro subscription, Pro becomes active in the app. What can be restored depends on Apple’s records. Restoring purchases never changes your bakery data, and importing a backup never adds or removes Pro.

Restoring a purchase does not restore orders. Move your bakery data using its separate backup and import tools.

Cancel

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone, Apple’s steps are: “In Settings, tap your name.”, “Tap Subscriptions.”, “Tap the subscription.”, “Tap Cancel Subscription.” See Apple Support: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by BakeTempo. Apple’s instructions start with “Sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com.” See Apple Support: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

“The subscription isn’t available right now”

Pro subscriptions and the free trial are not available yet, so the app cannot currently offer a purchase. Opening the Pro page or seeing this message does not start a purchase or create a charge. In the update with 5 free orders per calendar month, once those places are used, you must wait for the next monthly reset to create another free order. You can still view, share, export, back up and finish saved orders. An earlier TestFlight build may not have the free allowance yet. Keep your existing data and backups; do not delete or reinstall BakeTempo to try to make the subscription available. Contact support with your app version if you need help.

Good to know

  • BakeTempo is for iPhone and supports English and Simplified Chinese.
  • Each order belongs to one batch. Moving items between batches, partial pickups, recipes, inventory, invoices and online ordering by customers are not part of BakeTempo.
  • Pickup times keep the time zone they were saved in.
  • Read the Disclaimer and usage limits for food-safety, order-checking and backup responsibilities.
