BakeTempo

Plan, bake, pack and hand off. BakeTempo is an iPhone app for bakers who take orders and bake them in batches, at home or in a small kitchen.

What it does

  • Today shows the current batch at a glance: pieces planned, how many are made, orders, items packed and what’s next.
  • Orders lists every order by pickup day. Change a quantity, pickup time or note, or copy an order as a new one.
  • Bake shows how many of each item are needed, made, packed and still to make, with a simple checklist of steps.
  • Pack lets you pack each customer’s box against their order, in as many rounds as you need, then record the pickup when the whole order is collected.
  • Products is your menu, with a short specification, a built-in illustration and optional default steps for each item.

Changes after packing has started are marked for review, and corrections are separate, explicit steps with a short history. Production and packing lists can be shared as plain text, and all of My bake can be exported to a backup file you keep.

Your data

My bake is saved on your iPhone. BakeTempo has no account and no automatic cloud sync; you choose when to export a backup or share a list. Read the Privacy Policy.
