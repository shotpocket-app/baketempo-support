Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BakeTempo is an iPhone app for bakers who take orders. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website.

Summary

  • Your bakery data (products, batches, orders and the customer details you type in) is saved on your iPhone. BakeTempo has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • BakeTempo does not sync My bake to iCloud or any other cloud service. Your bakery data leaves the app when you export a backup or share a list, and your iPhone may include it in device backups you have turned on.
  • Apple handles BakeTempo Pro subscription purchases and related payment information. The developer does not receive your payment details.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code. This website uses no cookies, scripts or analytics.

Information you enter

You decide what to enter. BakeTempo stores:

  • Products: names, a short specification (for example “Standard” or “Small loaf”), the built-in illustration you pick, optional default steps, and whether a product is active.
  • Batches and production: batch names and dates, how many items are needed, made and packed, and step check-offs.
  • Free allowance: the 5-free-orders-per-calendar-month plan stores which newly created orders used free places, their calendar months and the allowance time zone. This information stays on your iPhone and may be included in your backups. It is not an Apple purchase receipt.
  • Orders: the customer name as you type it, pickup date, time and time zone, notes, item quantities, packing and pickup status, and a short history of changes to the order.

Customer names and notes can be personal information about your customers. Enter only what you need to run your orders, for example a first name and a short note.

Where it is stored

  • My bake is saved in BakeTempo’s storage on your iPhone. There is no automatic cloud sync of My bake.
  • Device backups: like other app data, BakeTempo’s data may be included in your iCloud Backup or in backups of your iPhone on a computer, if you have those turned on. Those backups are created and stored by Apple or by your computer under your own settings.

Backups, imports and sharing

These features handle your bakery data only when you use them:

  • Export backup creates one JSON file containing all of My bake, including customer names and notes, and saves it where you choose, for example in Files or a cloud drive you use. Anyone who can open that location can read the file, so keep backup files somewhere safe.
  • Import backup reads a backup file you select and shows what it holds before anything changes. If you choose to replace My bake, BakeTempo first keeps a copy of your previous My bake inside the app. That copy is not an outside backup.
  • Share a list passes a batch’s production list or a customer’s packing list as plain text to the app or person you pick in the iOS share sheet. A packing list includes the customer’s name, the pickup time and the order’s note. BakeTempo cannot know whether the text was received.

BakeTempo is not designed to upload your bakery data to the developer or to anyone else. Apple services that you use with the app, such as iCloud Backup, the App Store and the destinations you choose in the share sheet, work under Apple’s and those providers’ own terms.

Purchases (BakeTempo Pro)

  • Apple handles subscription purchases, trials, payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms and the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple determines the actual regional price and any introductory-offer eligibility shown before confirmation. The developer does not receive or store your payment details or your Apple Account password.
  • To decide whether Pro is active, the app asks Apple’s StoreKit on your iPhone for a verified, current BakeTempo Pro subscription, for example when the app starts, when it returns to the foreground and after a purchase or restore. The app also asks StoreKit whether your Apple Account can use an available free trial. These checks are about your subscription, not an upload of your bakery data.
  • BakeTempo does not save your Pro status in My bake or in backups, so importing or exporting a backup never adds or removes Pro.
  • Apple provides developers with sales and subscription reports and, only if you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, crash reports and usage statistics. The developer uses this information only to operate and improve BakeTempo.

What the app does not do

  • It does not show ads, sell information, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software development kits.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, scripts, analytics or embedded content. GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you contact us

Contact us at shotpocket6@gmail.com. If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and investigate the problem you report.

Keep customer information private. Use fictional names and redact screenshots. Do not email customer backups, Apple Account passwords, verification codes or payment details.

You can request deletion of a support conversation by contacting the same address. This does not delete data stored on your iPhone or files you exported. If you instead post to the public GitHub repository, anyone can read your post; GitHub handles that content under its own privacy statement.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Your BakeTempo data stays on your iPhone until you change it in the app, replace My bake with a backup, or delete the app. Cancelled orders and archived batches stay in My bake as history; the app has no separate “erase all” command.
  • Deleting BakeTempo removes its data from your iPhone. It does not remove backup files you exported, lists you shared, or copies in your iCloud or computer backups; manage those where you keep them.
  • Because your bakery data is not sent to the developer, the developer cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Keeping your own backups is your responsibility.

Children

BakeTempo is a tool for people who run baking orders. It is not directed at children.
