Disclaimer and usage limits

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BakeTempo helps you organize baking orders and record your work. It relies on the information and actions you enter.

Check each order before handing it over

Confirm customer details, quantities, pickup times and changes with your customer. Check the actual contents of each box before pickup. Marking an item made, packed or collected records your action; it does not verify what happened in your kitchen.

Food safety and allergies

BakeTempo does not assess food safety, allergens, cross-contact, storage temperatures, shelf life or nutritional information. Its checklists are your own workflow reminders. Check ingredients, labels and safe preparation practices independently, and follow the requirements that apply to your business.

What this app does not provide

BakeTempo does not take online customer orders, collect customer payments, produce invoices, track ingredient inventory or synchronize a team. Production totals are calculated from the orders and corrections you save; they are not an independent inventory count.

Keep your own backups

Your bakery data is stored on your iPhone. Export backups regularly and keep them in a safe place. Deleting the app or losing the device can remove data that you have not backed up. The developer cannot recover your local orders. Restoring an Apple purchase checks subscription access, not bakery records.

See backup and restore instructions and the Privacy Policy.

Free orders and Pro

Current TestFlight version 0.1 (5) includes 5 free orders per calendar month and direct Pro controls in Settings. Update through TestFlight without deleting the app to keep your saved bakery data. Pro is a monthly auto-renewable subscription for orders beyond the free allowance. The U.S. reference price is US$9.99 per month, with a 3-day free trial only when Apple confirms eligibility.

Pro offers appear when Apple returns an available subscription for your Apple Account and region. Review Apple’s actual regional price, trial offer and renewal terms before confirming. Installing BakeTempo does not start a subscription. An active subscription renews automatically unless cancelled. To avoid a charge after a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. See the full allowance, trial and subscription explanation.
